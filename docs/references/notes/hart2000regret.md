“Player i starts from a ‘‘reference point’’: his current actual play. His choice next period is governed by propensities to depart from it. It is natural therefore to postulate that, if a change occurs, it should be to actions that are perceived as being better, relative to the current choice.” ([Hart and Mas-Colell, 2000, p. 1131](zotero://select/library/items/R2XTLK78)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/J9BE3BFT?page=5&annotation=CAJV62GI))
