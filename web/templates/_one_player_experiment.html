Environment analysisEnvironment rule Current configuration

Punish the historical leaders

Before round t, the most frequent half of the actions over the complete history receive payoff 0; half is rounded up. Every other action receives payoff 1. Ties rotate deterministically.

The current sampled action never determines its own payoff vector. Bandit learners observe only the sampled payoff.

Independent lazy reward walks

Each action follows a precomputed random walk on the grid 0, 0.1, …, 1. Interior rewards move down, stay, or move up equally; boundaries stay or move inward equally.

The reward sequence depends only on the environment seed. The learner seed controls only action sampling.
